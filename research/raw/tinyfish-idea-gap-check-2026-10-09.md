# TinyFish idea-gap check — 2026-10-09

- **Construction dust:** TinyFish fetched The Hindu's 14 Jul 2026 report on Delhi Dust Portal 2.0. DPCC already maps construction sites, auto-assesses five dust-control parameters, finds the nearest monitor and emits yellow/orange/red alerts. A hackathon idea must add field-visit prioritisation or closure evidence rather than rebuild the portal. Source: https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Delhi/delhi-govt-launches-ai-powered-portal-to-tackle-pollution-from-construction-sites/article71217831.ece
- **Drought planning:** TinyFish fetched Hindustan Times' 20 Sep 2026 report on Maharashtra's circular. Districts must identify vulnerable villages using rainfall and groundwater, strengthen sources first, and submit plans on staged deadlines; tankers are a last resort. This supports a need-ranking or plan-audit product. Source: https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/mumbai-news/maharashtra-orders-early-water-scarcity-planning-makes-tankers-last-resort-101789845608365.html
- **Waste pickers:** TinyFish fetched PIB's 5 Jun 2025 NAMASTE announcement. The national Waste Picker Enumeration App already profiles workers and connects them to identity, health cards, PPE, skilling and collectives. A new idea should add an operational pickup, fair-price, or evidence handoff layer. Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2134303
- **Clean-air systems:** TinyFish fetched the 7 Sep 2026 PIB backgrounder. CAAQMS, SAMEER, PRANA, GRAP, OCEMS and dust-monitoring cells already form a large public stack. A new project should make one decision or accountability loop visible over those systems. Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?id=159871&NoteId=159871&ModuleId=3
