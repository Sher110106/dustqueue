  - WHO daily 15 ug/m3 exceeded on 99.8% of days. India 24h standard 60 ug/m3.
- DTE on State of Global Air 2025 (HEI + IHME), 2025-10-23, https://www.downtoearth.org.in/air/air-pollution-damaging-brain-health-worsening-disease-burden-in-india-soga-2025

  - Maharashtra, UP, Rajasthan, Bihar, West Bengal each >100,000 deaths in 2023.
  - Household air pollution deaths declining in India; ambient PM2.5 and ozone deaths rising. 75% of population lives where annual PM2.5 > WHO IT 35 ug/m3.

## Stubble detection research gap: geostationary satellites (opened)
- Jethva, "Timing the Flames: Geostationary Satellite Detection of Diurnally Shifting Stubble Burning in Northwestern India", Remote Sensing 2026, 18(10), 1506, published 11 May 2026, https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/18/10/1506
  - GK2A-AMI (Korean geostationary sat at 128.2E) hourly fire detection shows peak burn moved from 1-2 pm to 4-5 pm local time since 2022. MODIS/VIIRS (early afternoon passes) underreport.
  - Algorithm: 3.8 um SWIR vs 11.2 um IR contrast, applied 2019-2025. Limitation: ~4 km pixel over NW India due to slant view.
  - Operational AQ forecast models assimilate MODIS/VIIRS fire detections -> emissions underestimated.
  - Punjab fires in 12-18 IST window: ~6,000 -> 18,000 (2019-2021); 2024 ~49% decline vs earlier.
  - Uses MAIAC AOD 1 km (MCD19A2 V6.1) with relaxed QA for heavy smoke.
  - Data: GK2A-derived fire dataset for Punjab and Haryana on Zenodo https://zenodo.org/records/20084790 (not opened).
- (search snippet, X, not opened) Jethva: Punjab 2025 fires 23-57% higher than 2019, 2020, 2024; 17-28% lower than 2021-2023. https://x.com/hjethva05/with_replies
- (search snippet) ResearchGate Jun 2026: "Evidence of shift in stubble burning timing over northwest India from geostationary satellite observations" - active fire algorithm for INSAT-3rd generation (ISRO). https://www.researchgate.net/publication/397905183
- GK2A on AWS: Registry https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-gk2a-pds/ ; bucket s3://noaa-gk2a-pds (us-east-1, --no-sign-request), full disk every 10 min, 16 channels. SNS topic arn:aws:sns:us-east-1:709902155096:NewGK2AObject for new objects. NOAA NODD, open use with attribution.

- CEEW, "Custom Hiring Centres for Better Crop Residue Management: Insights from Punjab" (Jain, Kemanth, Sarao), 2025-09-16, https://www.ceew.in/publications/improving-access-to-crop-residue-management-solutions-with-custom-hiring-centres-in-agriculture
  - CRM scheme since 2018: >Rs 3,623 crore, ~2.95 lakh machines; ~40,000 CHCs in Punjab, Haryana, UP. Subsidy 50% individual, 80% CHC. MP added to scheme in 2023.

  - India >220 Mt surplus crop residue per year. Residue burning ~13% of India's PM2.5 emissions (TERI 2021).

## Forecast and source attribution tools (opened)
- CEEW, "How Well Can Delhi Predict Air Quality? Insights for India's Decision Support Systems" (Ignatious Sneha, Mohammad Rafiuddin, 2025), https://www.ceew.in/publications/designing-effective-air-quality-decision-support-and-pollution-warning-alert-systems

  - AQI data missing 13 days on AQEWS portal and 2 days on CPCB in 2024-25.
  - Recommends: GRAP impact display, district-sector contributions, updated emission inventories, year-round DSS, open data, ML bias correction.
