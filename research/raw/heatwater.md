## HEAT

### Official counts (opened)
- Lok Sabha USQ 2541, answered 4 Aug 2026 (MHA, Nityanand Rai). https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU2541_Ql0SOe.pdf?source=lsapps
  - 2026 (1 Mar - 26 Jul 2026): 4,853 heatstroke cases, 20 confirmed heatstroke deaths nationally (NHRIDS). Top cases: Telangana 915, West Bengal 733, Chhattisgarh 660, AP 540, Jharkhand 409, Maharashtra 325 (11 deaths), Bihar 249, UP 234, Rajasthan 204. Delhi 32 cases 0 deaths.
  - "National Heat-Related Illness and Death Surveillance (NHRIDS) activated for more than 51000 reporting units ... from all PHCs and above hospitals."
  - BIG POLICY CHANGE: 16th Finance Commission (award 2026-31) recommended heatwaves and lightning as notified natural calamities. MHA has included them under SDRF/NDRF Operational Guidelines for 2026-31. "Heat Wave Mitigation Projects are eligible under the Disaster Mitigation Funds." So heat relief money now has a legal channel (as of 2026).
- 2025: NCDC/IDSP via RTI: 7,192 suspected heatstroke cases, 14 confirmed deaths, 1 Mar - 24 Jun 2025 (snippets: ET Health, The Hindu, HeatWatch). HeatWatch news analysis counted at least 84 heatstroke deaths in 2025 (The Hindu snippet https://www.thehindu.com/sci-tech/health/india-recorded-at-least-84-heatstroke-deaths-in-2025-summer-study/article69964987.ece).
- 2024: NCDC ~41,789 suspected heatstroke cases, 143 deaths by ~21 Jun 2024 (Lancet Planetary Health snippet https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2542519624002468). NCRB 2024: 1,832 deaths from heat/sunstroke (isignal snippet https://weekly.isignal.in/p/the-trouble-with-indias-heatwave). Yale snippet: govt 459 heat-wave deaths in 2024 (IMD 2025); others 733.
- Undercount: official numbers are 1-2 orders of magnitude below modelled excess deaths.

### Excess mortality model (opened)
- Narang & Gadgil, Frontiers in Environmental Health, 26 May 2026. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/environmental-health/articles/10.3389/fenvh.2026.1789071/full
  - "a single day of extreme heat causes approximately 3,400 excess deaths nationally; a five-day heatwave causes nearly 30,000."
  - UP ~8,100 excess deaths in a 5-day heatwave; Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Surat each >250 in a single event.
  - "Only a small fraction of India's population (about 8%) has access to air conditioning."
  - HeatWatch found 733 heatstroke deaths in news Mar-Jul 2023 vs 360 official (MoHFW).

### 2026 heat season (opened)
- World Weather Attribution, April 2026 South Asia heat. https://www.worldweatherattribution.org/climate-change-exposes-hundreds-of-millions-to-longer-and-deadlier-pre-monsoon-heat-in-south-asia/
  - Mid-April into May 2026, Tmax >46C in many Indian cities. "At least 37 heat-related deaths were reported in India (The Wire, 2026)". Record power demand. Agricultural drought >1 million km2 (GDACS).
  - Event return period ~5 years now; climate change ~tripled probability; ~1C hotter.
  - "since heatwaves are not a notified/declared disaster in India" (WWA wrote this before the Aug 2026 MHA answer).
  - "dangerous humid heat is still rapidly rising" (aerosols + irrigation mask dry-bulb rise but raise humidity).
- BMJ / TheNewsMinute snippets: at least 16 heatstroke deaths in Telangana summer 2026.

### Heat Action Plan gaps (opened)
- CPR "How is India Adapting to Heatwaves?" (Pillai and Dalal, Mar 2023), via Deccan Herald https://www.deccanherald.com/science/indias-heatwave-action-plans-poorly-funded-fail-to-identify-vulnerable-groups-study-1204124.html
  - 37 HAPs reviewed. "Only 10 of the 37 HAPs ... establish locally-defined temperature thresholds". "only two of the 37 action plans explicitly carry out and present vulnerability assessments". 70% lack financial mechanisms; only 11 of 37 discuss funding; 8 of those ask departments to self-allocate. Many HAPs not online; asked NDMA for public repository.
- "Broken Signals" (Soumya Dutta, Nivarana 21 May 2025; Vikalp Sangam 12 Jun 2025) https://vikalpsangam.org/article/broken-signals-indias-heatwave-warnings-are-missing-the-mark/
  - IMD heatwave criteria: Tmax >=40C plains, >=37C coastal, >=30C hills; or departure >=4.5C; severe >=6.5C or >=45C.
  - 2024 had "a record breaking 554 heatwave days" (cites Delhi HAP 2025).
  - IMD stations often at airports (Guwahati station 25-30 km from centre). 2 m shaded air temp; asphalt/tin roofs reach 50-65C when air is 38-40C.
  - IMD "stops short of issuing formal Heat Index warnings". Alerts on smartphones, in English. Advice "stay indoors" impossible for daily wage workers.
  - Asks for: dense sensors in slums, real-time heat index / wet bulb, local-language voice/FM alerts, wage compensation, cooling rooms.
- IMD launched an experimental heat index ("feels like") in April 2023 (TOI snippet https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/imd-launches-heat-index-to-give-real-feel-information/articleshow/99856099.cms). UPSDMA did district-wise heat threshold study July 2024 (snippet https://upsdma.up.nic.in/2024/UPSDMA_HeatThreshold_19July2024.pdf).

### Outdoor / gig workers (opened)
- HeatWatch + Telangana Gig and Platform Workers Union survey, June 2024, Hyderabad. https://www.heatwatch.in/report/impact-of-extreme-heat-on-gig-workers-a-survey-report/
  - 52% heat exhaustion, 30% reported heat stroke. 65% want water+toilets, 55% shaded rest areas, 43% paid break in peak heat. Asked that rest-point locations be shown to workers inside the apps.
  - "over 11,868 heatstroke deaths were reported from 2012 to 2022".
- WRI India, Varun Agarwal, 28 May 2026. https://wri-india.org/perspectives/rising-heats-invisible-burden-delivery-workers
  - Riders: phones overheat and shut down -> orders cancelled, riders penalised. EV fast-charging pauses when battery hot -> up to an hour longer waits.
  - Maharashtra recently introduced SOPs for outdoor informal workers (staggered hours, compulsory breaks, shaded rest, hydration). Tamil Nadu Heat Mitigation Strategy.
- IFAT 2024 survey: over half of gig workers had heat symptoms (BusinessWorld / PolicyCircle snippets). BehanBox survey Sept 2026 on gig and domestic workers' heat costs (snippet https://behanbox.com/2026/09/07/gig-domestic-workers-are-absorbing-the-economic-costs-of-heat-stress-behanbox-survey/).

### Delhi hotspots and the engineer as user (opened)
- HT, 14 May 2026 https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/delhi-news/pwd-maps-448-waterlogging-hot-spots-ahead-of-monsoon-101778695835334.html

- Indian Express, 29 Aug 2026 https://indianexpress.com/article/delhi/delhi-pwd-inspect-139-waterlogging-hotspots-parvesh-sahib-singh-10854981/
  - After 24-25 Aug 2026 heavy rain (traffic paralysis, flight disruptions), PWD to inspect 139 new hotspots (113 found by traffic police up to 20 Aug, 26 more on 24 Aug); report per location: reasons, immediate measures, long-term fixes; checks drainage, outfall, siltation, pumps, encroachment.
- Snippets: Delhi govt identified 169 waterlogging hotspots (NIE, 25 Jun 2026); Traffic Police: 21 repeat spots among 72 locations flooded till 7 Aug 2026 (HT FB snippet). PWD SOP: manhole covers in 2 h, road repair in 48 h (TOI snippet).
- Insight: the hotspot list grows every year from traffic police reports after the fact. Engineers now carry personal blame. They need: before a storm, which of my N spots will flood first, which pumps go where; after, evidence of response time.

## WATER SUPPLY: TANKERS, NRW, GROUNDWATER

### Bengaluru 2024 crisis and tankers (opened)
- The Hindu, 5 Apr 2024 https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/water-woes-20-cut-in-consumption-the-only-way-bengaluru-can-sustain-this-summer/article68023220.ece
  - Demand 2,650 MLD; 1,450 MLD from Cauvery; rest groundwater. 6,900 of ~14,000 govt borewells dry. Price cap Rs 1,200 per 12,000 L tanker; operators still charged >Rs 2,000 in outer zones. Govt avoided hard crackdown to prevent artificial scarcity. Tankers haul from borewells in neighbouring districts. Bulk consumers (20 lakh to 2 crore L/day) use ~525 MLD; 20% cut for bulk, 10% for others.
- Al Jazeera (snippet): 6,900 of 13,900 borewells dry, some 457 m deep. Reuters (snippet, 21 Feb 2024): up to Rs 2,000 per 12,000 L.
- Sanchari Cauvery (BWSSB tanker service, launched May/Aug 2025): The Hindu snippet: Rs 1,290 for 12,000 L within 2 km + Rs 70/km. Moneycontrol snippet: 4,000 L Rs 660 ... 12,000 L Rs 1,290. Bangalore Mirror snippet: Rs 1.84 crore, 160 tankers with GPS + app. App "Kaverionwheels" https://kaverionwheels.bwssb.gov.in/ (Play Store com.bwssb.user.app).
- NIE, 5 Mar 2026 https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/bengaluru/2026/Mar/05/60-tankers-to-be-added-to-bengalurus-sanchari-cauvery-programme
  - Programme launched Aug 2025 for newly added 110 villages; 60 tankers, adding 60 more. North and East Bengaluru stressed every summer; South/West fine.
  - Private tanker association: demand down ~75%; now mostly PGs, hostels, apartments, gated communities. "If an apartment has around 180 houses, they require 12,000 litres of water which is supplied seven times a day."
  - Resident Varthur: private tankers cut prices after Sanchari Cauvery, but Feb-May spending still high.
- The Ken, 1 May 2026 https://the-ken.com/the-collection/bengaluru-doesnt-have-a-water-problem/ : pipes ~14,000 km supply ~1,450 MLD vs need ~2,100 MLD; gap via borewells + private tankers. Govt tanker app "didn't quite work". Pre-monsoon storm >100 mm in hours: wall collapse killed several incl. children (late April 2026).

### Bengaluru AI command centre (opened)
- Indian Express, 29 Apr 2026 https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/bangalore/bengaluru-to-launch-asias-largest-ai-command-centre-to-detect-water-leaks-predict-demand-in-real-time-10661535/

### Delhi Jal Board (opened)
- Pioneer, 25 Mar 2026, CAG Performance Audit Report No. 3 of 2025 (tabled 23 Mar 2026, period 2017-22) https://dailypioneer.com/news/20-million-lives-at-risk-cag-delivers-damning-verdict-on-delhis-worsening-water-crisis

- SANDRP, 6 Apr 2026 https://sandrp.in/2026/04/06/drp-6-april-2026-cag-report-on-djb-indicates-fundamental-issues/

- ORF, Dhaval Desai, 24 Aug 2025 https://www.orfonline.org/expert-speak/the-cost-of-leakage-how-water-losses-fuel-unsustainable-choices

Chennai NRW up to 30%, gap 1,200 MLD. ~75% of NRW in South Asian cities is physical leakage. Only ~50% of Indian urban households have working water meters. Intermittent supply -> pipes depressurise -> contaminated seepage.

### Existing city flood warning systems (opened)
- Business Standard, 9 Jul 2026 https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/how-urban-flood-warning-systems-work-mumbai-chennai-show-the-way-126070900246_1.html
  - iFLOWS-Mumbai (MoES + BMC, June 2020): 24 wards; seven modules; inundation 3 days ahead; 3-6 h nowcast; ~12 h location flood prediction; dissemination to field officials.
  - Chennai Real-Time Flood Forecasting and Spatial DSS (after 2015 floods, World Bank, IIT Madras): 72 h, five weather models, 74 watersheds, 989 km rivers, reservoir guidance.
  - "IMD's Vision 2047 document says urban flood warning systems are operational in Mumbai and Chennai and proposes extending them to other major cities in phases." So most cities (Delhi, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Pune, Patna...) have none.
  - World Bank 2025: ~318,000 km of urban roads (27% of India's urban road network) exposed to a 1-in-100-year flood.
- Citizen Matters, 25 Jul 2025, Bengaluru https://citizenmatters.in/bengalurus-flood-alert-system-good-for-rescue-not-prevention/
  - KSNDMC + BBMP: 201 flood-prone areas; 100 telemetric rain gauges (15-min), 12 weather stations, 105 radar water-level sensors on stormwater drains, IP cameras for garbage. Colour code: yellow <=25%, orange 50%, red 80% full.
  - "Data ... is currently being used only for rescue and evacuation." BBMP AEE: drains built for max 80 mm; beyond that "it will flood regardless". Pumps only after flooding starts. Alerts to zonal EEs 6-8 h ahead (in theory).
  - Varuna Mitra + Megha Sandesha apps: "only about 10,000 downloads within five years" (WELL Labs hydrologist). RWA member: people next to sensors neither know nor get alerts.
  - Example of success: Feb sensor showed 80% in Nagarbhavi drain in mild rain -> garbage blockage -> cleared before flooding.
  - BBMP worked on 4 of 53 flood-prone areas; World Bank funds for 22 of remaining 49.
  - Agartala uses sensors + 9 lakh L/h pumps to prevent floods.
- Mumbai crowd reporting site exists: https://www.mumbaiflood.in ("Spotted flooding in your area? ... report it") (snippet only).

## DATASETS AND ACCESS (verified by me where marked)

- IMD API Management Platform https://api.imd.gov.in/public/index.php ; reference https://api.imd.gov.in/public/api_reference.html ; guide https://api.imd.gov.in/public/IMD_API_Portal_User_Guide.pdf (opened)
  - Needs account; API key bound to a STATIC PUBLIC IP of the calling server (max 2 DEV + 2 PROD keys); plus JWT from POST /api/oauth/token.php (expires 3600 s). Headers X-API-KEY + Authorization: Bearer. Approval time not stated. Govt orgs use official email.
  - Endpoints: cityforecast (7-day, Tmax/Tmin, RH 0830/1730), current_wx, districtnowcast (3-hourly cats incl. heavy rain >15 mm/h, colour 1-4, toi/Vupto), districtrainfall, districtwarning (5-day codes incl. 9 Heat Wave, 10 Hot Day, 11 Warm Night, 2 Heavy Rain, 16 Very Heavy, 17 Extremely Heavy), stationnowcast, aws_data (AWS/ARG stations, per state, with CURR_TEMP, DEW_POINT_TEMP, RH, and "Feel Like"), basinqpf, subdivision/district rainfall forecast, RADAR & lightning, agromet.
  - Hackathon note: IP binding means calls must come from a fixed IP (e.g. EC2 Elastic IP or NAT gateway). Risky if signup approval is slow. Visual pages (mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/districtWiseNowcastGIS.php etc.) are public.
- IMD gridded (IMD Pune): daily rainfall 0.25 deg 1901-2024 (NetCDF page https://www.imdpune.gov.in/cmpg/Griddata/Rainfall_25_NetCDF.html, snippet); Tmax/Tmin 1 deg archive; real-time rain 0.25, real-time temp 0.5 (imdR manual snippet). Python: imdlib (https://imdlib.readthedocs.io). No key.
