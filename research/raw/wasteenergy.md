## SWM Rules 2026 (PIB, 28 Jan 2026) https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2219676
- Supersede SWM Rules 2016. In force 1 April 2026.
- Four-stream segregation at source mandatory: wet, dry, sanitary, special care.
- Bulk Waste Generator (BWG): floor area >=20,000 sq m, or water use >=40,000 L/day, or waste >=100 kg/day. Includes govt depts, institutions, commercial, residential societies.
- EBWGR (Extended Bulk Waste Generator Responsibility): process wet waste on site or get an EBWGR certificate. "bulk waste generators account for nearly 30 per cent of total solid waste generation."
- Environmental compensation (polluter pays) for no registration, false reporting, forged documents.
- CPCB "Centralised Online Portal" to track generation, collection, transport, processing, disposal, biomining. Facility audits uploaded on portal. Quarterly legacy dumpsite progress via portal.
- MRFs formally recognised; can be deposit points for e-waste, special care, sanitary waste.
- RDF substitution in cement/WtE: 5% rising to 15% over six years.
- Higher landfill fee for unsegregated waste, above cost of segregation+processing.

## Synthesis (my reading, not sourced facts)
Underserved, decision-shaped problems with a clear user, ranked by fit for a 2-day build:
1. BWG / RWA compliance under SWM Rules 2026 (Delhi). User: RWA manager or facility manager of a society/school/hospital. Decision: what to fix this week so MCD does not refuse pickup or fine us; how much wet waste to process on site vs buy EBWGR; what goes in the 30 June return. Fresh rules (in force Apr 2026; CPCB registration guidelines Sep 2026), MCD empanelling partners, only 3,749 BWGs registered in 2025. Very few repos. Risk: looks like paperwork; must show waste diverted.
2. Landfill methane/fire watch (Ghazipur, Okhla, Bhalswa). User: MCD landfill site engineer / DPCC / Basti Suraksha Manch. Decision: which face of the mound to cover/soak/avoid this week; is biomining raising emissions. Carbon Mapper API has 69 plumes over the three sites through May 2026. Risk: plumes are sparse in time (not daily); user access hard; keep it a weekly brief.
3. Rooftop solar for Delhi before PMSG closes (28 Feb 2027). User: household with an application stuck, or RWA. Decision: next step on a stuck application (vendor holding >60 days, inspection pending, switch vendor), or which pathway (CAPEX / RESCO / VNM) for flats. Data: division CSV (78,012 applications), deactivated vendor list, NASA POWER / GSA. Risk: SolarLens, SunSense, dg-solar-advisor already exist; starter idea "payback by pincode" crowded.
4. Waste picker inclusion. User: picker via NGO (Chintan, SEWA). Decision: where to sell / which MRF; how to get NAMASTE ID. Risk: low-literacy users; needs voice + NGO; demo hard to make real.
5. DEVi last-mile reliability. User: DTC depot/route manager or commuter. Data: OTD GTFS-RT (key). Risk: key approval time; static GTFS stale.
Crowded: waste photo classifier / bin sorter; household scrap pickup marketplace; carbon footprint calculator; EV charger finder; solar payback calculator; RAG chatbot over SWM rules; complaint apps; food waste apps.
